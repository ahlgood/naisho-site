Privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Naisho (内緒, “a secret, just between us”) is a private AI chat app for iPhone, made by Dr. Paul Hersh, LMFT. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information. Questions: drpaul@drpaulhersh.com.

The short version

  • The AI runs on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device model.
  • Your chats are stored only on your iPhone. We never receive them.
  • There's no account. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number in the app.
  • The app works offline and has no ads and no analytics code.
  • You can delete all your chats at any time.

Your chats

What you type and Hoshi's replies are processed by Apple's on-device language model (part of Apple Intelligence) on your iPhone. Naisho doesn't send your conversations to us or to any other server.

Chats are saved in the app's storage on your iPhone. Each conversation is encrypted with a key kept in your iPhone's Keychain for this device only, and the chat files are excluded from iCloud and computer backups. Because of this, chats can't be restored onto a new phone, and deleting the app deletes them.

To erase everything, use the trash button on the Chats screen or “Delete all chats” in the privacy screen. After you confirm, every conversation is removed from your phone.

Feedback

If you tap the feedback button, Naisho opens a draft email in your own mail app. Nothing is sent unless you send it. If you do, we receive that email and use it only to improve the app.

TestFlight beta

The beta is delivered through Apple's TestFlight. Apple shares some beta information with developers, such as whether the app was installed, how many sessions it had, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send through TestFlight. If you joined with the public link, Apple doesn't show us your name or email. Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Beta update emails

If you sign up for beta updates on this website, your email address is stored with Kit, the email service we use, and used only to email you about new beta builds. You'll get a confirmation email first, every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your address anytime at drpaul@drpaulhersh.com. We don't sell or share your email.

This website

This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics, ads or tracking scripts. Like any website, the hosting service may process standard request information (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and protect the site.

Not therapy, not for emergencies

Naisho is a private place to think out loud. It isn't therapy or medical care. If you're in crisis in the US, call or text 988. If you're in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
