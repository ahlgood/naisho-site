Questions

Is Naisho therapy?

No. Naisho is a private place to put your thoughts into words. It isn't therapy, counseling or medical care, and it can't diagnose or treat anything. If you'd like support from a person, a licensed professional is the right call.

Where are my chats stored?

Only on your iPhone. Each chat is encrypted with a key kept in your iPhone's Keychain for this device only, and chats are left out of iCloud backups. There's no Naisho server and no account, so we can't see your chats. That also means if you delete the app or change phones, your chats don't come with you.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The AI runs on your iPhone using Apple's on-device model, so Naisho works in airplane mode. You only need a connection to install it.

Which iPhones does it work on?

An iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, or iPhone 16 or later, on iOS 26, with Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri). On older iPhones the app installs but can't chat. Right after you turn Apple Intelligence on, your iPhone may need a few minutes on Wi-Fi to finish downloading Apple's model.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Naisho is a free beta on TestFlight right now.

How do I delete my chats?

Tap the trash button on the Chats screen, or use “Delete all chats” in the privacy screen. You confirm once, and every conversation is erased from your phone.

What if I'm in crisis?

Please don't rely on Naisho. In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), any time, day or night. If you're in immediate danger, call 911. Outside the US, contact your local emergency number.

Why does the AI sometimes say no?

Naisho uses Apple's built-in on-device AI, which has its own safety filter. Now and then it turns down an ordinary request. Tap Try again, or Rephrase and say it a little differently.
